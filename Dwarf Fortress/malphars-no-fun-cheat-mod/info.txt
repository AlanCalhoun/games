[NAME:Malphar's No Fun Cheat Mod]
[DESCRIPTION:An exercise in whether this kind of mod could be made, not whether it should. For when you don't want to have any fun. Extreme cheat mod for Dwarf Fortress with DFHack. Enable this mod by itself and generate a new world. Dwarves do not eat, drink, sleep, or breathe, and they do not feel pain, fear, stun, or exertion. They start at legendary skill and finish jobs instantly. Buildings place without stocked materials: open the build menu and the mod puts an adamantine item on the cursor tile, or the best material that item can be. Choose Closest item. A door still has to sit in a gap next to a wall. Loose boulders and rough gems stay hidden until a stockpile, and they still occupy the tile. No aquifers. Plants, food, and corpses do not rot, and miasma is cleared. MultiHaul hauls many items with a wheelbarrow. Malphar's Cheat Workshop groups jobs into Food, Hospital, Furniture, Weapons, Armor and clothes, Materials, and Tools. Each job conjures a full set at masterwork quality. The best meat, prepared fish, cheese, lavish meals, and drink are kept in supply and replaced when used. The best clothes and personal items are kept the same way: shirts, cloaks, pants, shoes, gloves, hoods, armor, backpacks, flasks, pouches, and jewelry, replaced when worn. Animal fodder is kept too: plump helmets, cave wheat, pig tails, sweet pods, and their seeds. Animal corpses, bones, shells, skins, and other refuse are removed. Corpses of dwarves, humans, elves, and other people are left for burial. Soap, cloth, medical gear, and an army kit also appear on the ground. Cloth is giant cave spider silk, leather is roc, and soap is rock nut. Drink is barreled. Source: https://github.com/AlanCalhoun/games/tree/main/Dwarf%20Fortress/malphars-no-fun-cheat-mod]
