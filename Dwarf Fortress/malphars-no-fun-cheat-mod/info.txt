[NAME:Malphar's No Fun Cheat Mod]
[DESCRIPTION:An exercise in whether this kind of mod could be made, not whether it should. For when you don't want to have any fun. Extreme cheat mod for Dwarf Fortress with DFHack. Enable this mod by itself and generate a new world. Dwarves do not eat, drink, sleep, or breathe, and they do not feel pain, fear, stun, or exertion. They start at legendary skill and finish jobs instantly. Buildings place without stocked materials. Loose boulders and rough gems stay hidden until a stockpile. No aquifers. No miasma. Malphar's Cheat Workshop conjures masterwork sets. Best food, drink, clothes, and fodder stay in supply with soft caps. Stockpiles share one inventory; goods teleport into infinite bins and barrels without dwarf hauling. Stuck citizens teleport to the wagon or trade depot. Production mandates complete automatically. Screw pumps, millstones, and rollers are self-powered with no axles; a lever on a nearby gear toggles them. At most one short-lived ghost. Animal refuse is removed; people stay for burial. Source: https://github.com/AlanCalhoun/games/tree/main/Dwarf%20Fortress/malphars-no-fun-cheat-mod]
